# About Chingiringi

Chingiringi is a smarter way to discover products, explore deals, shop, and earn rewards.

We bring shopping, product discovery, short-form videos, local stores, and rewards together in one simple platform.

Discover products through engaging videos, explore deals and offers, and shop from our partner platforms. You can also discover physical stores around you and explore products and businesses in your area.

With Chingiringi, users can also earn CR Coins through eligible activities such as sharing products, deals, and content. These rewards can be accumulated in your Chingiringi wallet and, where applicable, redeemed or withdrawn according to our reward terms.

Our goal is simple — make discovering and shopping more rewarding.

Whether you're looking for your next purchase, discovering a new brand, exploring local businesses, or sharing products you love, Chingiringi is built to make the experience more engaging and rewarding.

**Discover. Shop. Share. Earn. — That's Chingiringi.**
