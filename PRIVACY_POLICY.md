## 1. Scope

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the categories of data below. Categories marked **(sensitive)** receive additional protection.

### 2.1 Personal information you provide

- **Identity and contact data:** name, username, email address, and/or mobile number, and a profile photo (avatar) if you add one.
- **Account authentication data:** a password (stored only as a secure hash) if you register with one, a Google account identifier if you sign in with Google, and one-time passwords (OTPs) used to verify your phone or email.
- **Delivery addresses:** if you save one — full name, phone number, address lines, city, state, and pincode.
- **Financial information (sensitive):** to pay out CR Coins in INR, we collect the bank account number and IFSC, or the UPI ID, and the account-holder name, together with any verification information required to complete a payout. We do **not** collect or store full card numbers.
- **User content:** videos, images, reviews, captions, links, and comments you upload or submit.
- **Support communications:** information you provide when you contact us.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

- **App activity and interactions:** products, deals, stores, and videos you view or interact with; shares you make; searches; and rewards-related activity.
- **Device and log data:** device type, operating system, platform, app version, IP address, user agent, and referring URL. We record IP address, user agent, and referring URL when you click affiliate links or open shared links (including, in some cases, before you sign in) so that activity can be attributed and reconciled.
- **Approximate or precise location (sensitive):** only if you grant location permission, so we can show physical stores and offers near you. You control this through your device settings.

### 2.3 Data from third parties

- **Sign-in providers:** when you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name, email, and a Google account identifier) in line with your Google account settings.
- **Merchants and affiliate networks:** transaction and order information (such as whether a qualifying purchase occurred, order value, and status) used to determine cashback and rewards eligibility.
- **Referrals:** if you join through a referral, we associate your account with the referring user's referral code.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create, authenticate, and manage your account.
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Platform and its features.
- Display products, deals, stores, videos, and personalized or nearby content.
- Track eligible clicks, shares, purchases, and activities to calculate, credit, and manage cashback, rewards, and CR Coins.
- Process withdrawals of CR Coins into INR and complete related payouts.
- Send transactional and service messages, including push notifications about cashback, wallet activity, withdrawals, and shares, subject to your preferences.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, self-referrals, reward manipulation, and other prohibited or unlawful activity.
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
- Comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

We do not use your data for any purpose that is unrelated to the features described in the App without obtaining your consent.

## 4. Cashback, Rewards, and Attribution

To attribute cashback and rewards, we log affiliate-link clicks and shared-link opens. These records may include your user account (where available), the merchant, the links involved, a tracking identifier (subid), and technical data such as IP address, user agent, and referring URL.

Where a merchant report does not include our tracking identifier, we may match a transaction to your activity using signals such as merchant, timestamp, and amount. We use this information to determine reward eligibility and to detect invalid, duplicate, or fraudulent activity.

## 5. Payments and Withdrawals

Withdrawals of CR Coins into INR are processed through third-party payment and payout providers (such as Cashfree, and previously Razorpay). We share the payout details necessary to complete your withdrawal — such as the account-holder name and the destination bank account and IFSC, or the UPI ID — with the applicable provider. These providers process your information under their own terms and privacy policies. We retain records of withdrawals and payouts for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and legal-compliance purposes.

## 6. User Content

Content you upload — such as videos, images, and reviews — may be stored and delivered through third-party media and streaming providers (such as Cloudinary, Cloudflare Stream, and Mux) and, once approved where moderation applies, may be visible to other users of the Platform. Please be mindful of the information you include in content you make public. You are responsible for the content you upload, as described in our Terms & Conditions.

## 7. Location Information

If you grant location permission, we use your device location to show nearby stores and relevant offers. We request location access in the normal use of the relevant feature, and you can enable or disable it at any time through your device settings. Disabling location may limit location-based features. We do not use location data for advertising, and we do not share precise location with third parties for their own purposes.

## 8. Notifications and Communications

With your permission, we register a push-notification token for your device to send you notifications (for example, about cashback, wallet activity, withdrawals, and shares). You can manage notification preferences within the App and disable notifications through your device settings.
