# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chingiringi ("Chingiringi", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you access or use the Chingiringi mobile application, website, and related services ("Platform").

This Policy should be read together with our [Terms & Conditions](TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md). By using Chingiringi, you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Platform.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to information we process about users of the Platform. It does not apply to the independent practices of merchants, affiliate networks, payment providers, or other third parties whose services you may access through Chingiringi. Those third parties have their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

- **Account information:** name, username, email address, and/or mobile number, and a profile photo (avatar) if you choose to add one.
- **Authentication information:** a password (stored only as a secure hash) if you register with one, or a Google account identifier if you sign in with Google, and one-time passwords (OTPs) used to verify your phone or email.
- **Delivery addresses:** where you choose to save one, your full name, phone number, address lines, city, state, and pincode.
- **Withdrawal and payout information:** to pay out CR Coins in INR, we collect the bank account number and IFSC, or the UPI ID, and the account-holder name you provide, along with any verification information required for the payout.
- **User-generated content:** videos, images, reviews, captions, links, comments, and other content you upload or submit.
- **Communications:** information you provide when you contact support or otherwise communicate with us.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device and app information:** device type, operating system, platform, app version, and similar technical identifiers.
- **Log and connection data:** IP address, user agent, and referring URL, which we record when you click affiliate links, open shared links, or otherwise interact with the Platform.
- **Usage and activity data:** products, deals, stores, and videos you view or interact with; shares you make; and rewards-related activity.
- **Approximate or precise location:** where you grant permission, so we can show physical stores and offers available near you. You can control this through your device settings.

### 2.3 Information from third parties

- **Sign-in providers:** when you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name, email, and a Google account identifier) in accordance with your Google account settings.
- **Merchants and affiliate networks:** transaction and order information (such as whether a qualifying purchase occurred, order value, and status) used to determine cashback and rewards eligibility.
- **Referrals:** if you join through a referral, we associate your account with the referring user's referral code.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- Operate, maintain, and improve the Platform and its features.
- Display products, deals, stores, videos, and personalized or nearby content.
- Track eligible clicks, shares, purchases, and activities so we can calculate, credit, and manage cashback, rewards, and CR Coins.
- Process withdrawals of CR Coins into INR and complete related payouts.
- Send transactional and service messages, including push notifications about cashback, rewards, withdrawals, and shares, subject to your preferences.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, self-referrals, reward manipulation, and other prohibited or unlawful activity.
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
- Comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

## 4. Cashback, Rewards, and Attribution

To attribute cashback and rewards, we log affiliate-link clicks and shared-link opens. These records may include your user account (where available), the merchant, the links involved, a tracking identifier (subid), and technical data such as IP address, user agent, and referring URL.

Where a merchant report does not include our tracking identifier, we may match a transaction to your activity using signals such as merchant, timestamp, and amount. Some click and share records may be created even where a user is not signed in, so that activity can be reconciled if you later sign in.

We use this information to determine reward eligibility and to detect invalid, duplicate, or fraudulent activity.

## 5. Payments and Withdrawals

Withdrawals of CR Coins into INR are processed through third-party payment and payout providers (such as Cashfree, and previously Razorpay). We share the payout details necessary to complete your withdrawal — such as the account-holder name and the destination bank account and IFSC or UPI ID — with the applicable provider.

These providers process your information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also retain records of withdrawals and payouts for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and legal-compliance purposes.

## 6. User-Generated Content

Content you upload — such as videos, images, and reviews — may be stored and delivered through third-party media and streaming providers (such as Cloudinary, Cloudflare Stream, and Mux) and, once approved where moderation applies, may be visible to other users of the Platform.

Please be mindful of the information you choose to include in content you make public. You are responsible for the content you upload, as described in our Terms & Conditions.

## 7. Location Information

If you grant location permission, we use your location to show nearby stores and relevant offers. You can enable or disable location access at any time through your device settings. Disabling location may limit location-based features.

## 8. Notifications and Communications

With your permission, we register a push-notification token for your device to send you notifications (for example, about cashback, wallet activity, withdrawals, and shares). You can manage notification preferences within the app and disable notifications through your device settings.

## 9. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

- **Service providers:** with vendors who process information on our behalf — such as cloud hosting and database providers, authentication providers, payment and payout providers, media and video providers, and notification providers — under appropriate obligations.
- **Merchants and affiliate networks:** as needed to track and validate purchases and rewards; this may include a tracking identifier passed with affiliate links.
- **Legal and safety:** where we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, or a governmental request, or is necessary to protect the rights, safety, property, or security of Chingiringi, our users, or others, or to prevent fraud or abuse.
- **Business transfers:** in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.
- **With your direction:** where you ask us to share information or make content public.

## 10. Third-Party Services and Links

The Platform may contain links to, or integrations with, third-party websites, merchants, stores, and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing information to them.

## 11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We and our providers may use cookies, device identifiers, tracking parameters (such as subid), and similar technologies to operate the Platform, remember your session, attribute rewards, prevent fraud, and understand usage. Where the Platform is accessed on the web, you can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect functionality.

## 12. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform, and thereafter as necessary to comply with our legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements. Some records — such as transaction, payout, and fraud-prevention logs — may be retained for longer periods where required. OTPs and certain temporary records are deleted automatically after they expire.

## 13. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. For example, passwords and OTPs are stored as secure hashes rather than in plain text, authentication credentials on your device are kept in secure storage, and data in transit is protected using encryption.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your account credentials confidential and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.
